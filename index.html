Where you are

Filled boxes are the path you have taken; the outlined box is where you are now. Everything faded is a road not taken — scroll around to see where the other answers would have led.

This tool asks the questions in the order that makes each next question answerable. It is a way of thinking, not an oracle — if the answer it gives you feels wrong, the interesting thing is why, and that is worth raising in class.

Rebuilt from the course's research design flowchart and the causal inference chooser.
